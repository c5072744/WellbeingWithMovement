Welcome to Wellbeing with Movement!

This app is intended to provide some tailored activity advice to people who have had cancer. You can answer a few questions and get an activity programme that is geared to your current abilities.

This site is part of a student project. This is not medical advice. Check with a healthcare provider before starting something new.

Who am I?

My name is Cindy Forbes. I have been a researcher in physical activity and its effects on cancer for 15 years. My research focuses on how to best deliver and embed wellbeing programmes into standard care for people with cancer. I also look at how digital technology can help this delivery.
