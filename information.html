Sources for more support

Below are links to trusted sources for information on cancer and physical activity. You can read about your specific type of cancer and what activity could be helpful for you.

Macmillan

Macmillan is a national charity across the UK with the goal of doing whatever it takes to help people get the best care they can. They are well-known for supporting being active before (prehabilitation), during, and after (rehabilitation) cancer treatments. Follow the link below to get more information and support on any topic.

Yorkshire Cancer Research

Yorkshire Cancer Research is a Yorkshire specific charity that aims to help more people in Yorkshire affected by cancer live longer healthier lives.

Learn more about the charity's exercise research here. Exercise and Cancer in Yorkshire

With researchers at Sheffield Hallam University, they developed and embedded a service offering free fitness, nutrition, and wellbeing support to people after a cancer diagnosis.

You can learn more about this service, Active Together, here. Active Together


Physical activity guidelines for people with cancer

In 2019 an international group of organisations published updated exercise guidelines for cancer survivors (Campbell KL et al. Exercise Guidelines for Cancer Survivors. doi: 10.1249/MSS.0000000000002116.)

Effects of Moderate to Vigorous intensity Exercise on Health-Related Outcomes in People with Cancer. Adapted from Campbell et al. 2019.
Outcomes Cardio Only Strength Only Both Cardio and Strength
Cancer-related fatigue 3x per week; 30 mins each, moderate intensity 2x per week; 2 sets, 12-15 reps, moderate intensity 3x 30-min cardio & 2 strength per week (2 sets, 12-15 reps), moderate intensity
Health-Related Quality of Life 2-3x per week; 30-60 mins each, moderate to vigorous intensity 2x per week; 2 sets, 8-15 reps, moderate to vigorous intensity 2-3x 20-30 min cardio & 2-3x strength per week (2 sets, 8-15 reps), moderate to vigorous intensity
Physical Function 3x per week; 30-60 mins each, moderate to vigorous intensity 2-3x per week; 2 sets, 8-12 reps, moderate to vigorous intensity 3x 20-40 min cardio & 2-3x strength per week (2 sets, 8-12 reps), moderate to vigorous intensity
Anxiety or Depression 3x per week; 30-60 mins each, moderate to vigorous intensity Not enough strong evidence. 2-3 20-40 min cardio & 2-3x strength per week (2 sets, 8-12 reps), moderate to vigorous intensity
Lymphoedema Not enough strong evidence. 2-3 sessions per week of appropriate exercises do not exacerbate lymphoedema Not enough strong evidence.
